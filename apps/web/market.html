The wedge

Provenance Report on
existing corpora

A buyer (or a supplier closing a deal) sends a LeRobot v3 dataset. We ingest it, commit every episode to the log, run L3 checks (deduplication, jitter, plausibility, sim detection), anchor, and deliver a counsel-readable report with proofs and an on-chain receipt.

The customer

A lab that has been quoted 10k episodes by a capture vendor and needs counsel to sign off. Or a supplier trying to close an enterprise deal. Either way: somebody whose purchase or sale becomes defensible with a report they can check without trusting us.

What gets delivered

A JSON report with full proofs (inclusion, consistency, consent non-membership), a PDF for counsel, and an on-chain receipt naming the corpus root, terms hash, and anchor. Every episode shows its L0 badge (committed, timestamps, log position, consent status) and its L3 badges (checks run, thresholds, results).

Why this is winnable now

Because it needs no hardware, no SDK, no contributors, and no simulator. The report uses data that already exists. Every report is a reference corpus that immediately improves the next verification. And it retires the fiction that real data has never been logged.

Revenue and reach

$5–25k per report. First customer: any of the ~30 labs training manipulation policies; direct outreach by name. Weeks to first dollar. Strategic value: track record + first datasets logged = the moat that later becomes a standard.

The Recorder SDK follows

Later: sign at capture

A lerobot recorder plugin that generates a session key, signs each episode's manifest, and streams leaves to the log. Optional device attestation (Android Key Attestation, TPM). Suppliers use this to be pre-verified so buyers skip the report stage. Where L1 and L2 claims originate.

Pricing shape

Three revenue streams,
no invented numbers

Provenance Report

Fixed fee per corpus. Covers ingest, compute, delivery, and counsel reading time.

Per-episode logging (SDK phase)

SaaS on the Recorder SDK. Includes submission, storage, on-chain commitment, and audit trail. Suppliers pass this through to their own customers.

Settlement take

2.5% of USDC licence payments. Buyer pays 100 USDC for a corpus, 97.5 reaches the supplier (or is split per the receipt), 2.5 funds the log operator and mirrors.
