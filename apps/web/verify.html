The log on chain

Check it yourself.

A buyer needs to verify a slice of capture data without trusting our internal records, and a contributor needs to withdraw without us being able to pretend they did not. Pick any chain THENAR is anchored on, or point this page at your own RPC. Nothing here passes through our servers.

reading the chain…

Paste any preimage the log holds — 154 bytes (0x01 clip), 197 (0x02 episode), 145 (0x03 corpus manifest) or 141 (0x04 verification claim) — its position, and the sibling path. This page checks it two ways: against the chosen chain's LeafVerifier, and again entirely on your own machine with no network at all.

Paste a report JSON, or link one with ?report=<url>. This runs PLAN §10.10 steps 3–7 in the browser: log inclusion, the consistency proof from the corpus's sealing anchor to this report's anchor, consent status, every claim, and corpus inclusion. Step 1 (file hashes) cannot run here — this page never has the delivered files — and says so rather than skipping silently.

Prove one episode leaf belongs to a sealed corpus, against the corpus's own root — the same tree sealCorpus commits to on chain (PLAN §10.7).
