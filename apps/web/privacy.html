Privacy

Last updated 20 August 2026.

Thenar is a small company building capture hardware. This page says what we hold and why, in the plainest terms we can manage.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form on this site: your name, your email address, what you do, and whatever you write in the message field. Nothing else. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, and no cookie set by this site, which is why you are not being asked to dismiss a consent banner.

Why we hold it

To reply to you, and to decide what to build first. We do not sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list. If you tell us which robot tasks are failing, we may use that to prioritise what we instrument, in aggregate and without your name attached.

Who else sees it

The message is delivered by Resend, our email provider, and lands in a normal inbox. That is the whole chain.

Capture data

The contributor network described elsewhere on this site does not exist yet and has collected nothing from anybody. When it does, consent and licence terms will be recorded with each contribution, personal data will stay off any public ledger, and this page will be rewritten before a single recording is taken.

Deleting it

Write to hello@thenar.io and we will delete your message and your details. You do not need to give a reason and we will not ask for one.
